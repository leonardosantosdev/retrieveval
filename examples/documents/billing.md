# Billing and payment methods

Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing period and charged to the
payment method on file. You can view every past invoice under
**Account → Billing → Invoices**.

## Updating your card

Go to **Account → Billing → Payment method** and enter the new card details.
The change applies to the next invoice; an invoice that has already failed is
not retried automatically until you trigger a retry.

## Failed payments

When a charge is declined we retry it after 3 days and again after 7 days.
The workspace enters a restricted state after the third failure: existing
data stays readable but new writes are blocked until payment succeeds.

## Tax and VAT

Add your VAT or tax identification number under **Account → Billing → Tax
details** before an invoice is issued. Invoices already issued cannot be
amended to add a tax number retroactively.

## Changing plan

Upgrading applies immediately and the current period is prorated.
Downgrading applies at the start of the next period.
