# Cancelling your subscription

You can end your subscription at any point from the account area. Open
**Account → Plan** and choose *End plan*. The change takes effect at the close
of the current billing period, so you keep access to every paid feature until
that date.

## What happens to your data

Your workspace stays intact for 90 days after the plan ends. During that
window you can start a new plan and pick up exactly where you left off.
After 90 days the workspace and its contents are permanently removed and
cannot be restored.

## Annual plans

An annual plan can be ended early, but the remaining months are not refunded
automatically. If you ended an annual plan by mistake within the first 14
days, contact support and reference your invoice number for a full refund.

## Reactivating

Starting a new plan on the same workspace restores your previous settings,
integrations and team members. Billing restarts on the day you reactivate.
