# Team members and permissions

A workspace has three roles: owner, admin and member. There is exactly one
owner at a time, and ownership can be transferred but not shared.

## What each role can do

Owners and admins can invite people, change roles and manage billing. Members
can read and write content but cannot see billing details or change anyone's
role. Only the owner can delete the workspace or transfer ownership.

## Inviting people

Invitations are sent by email and expire after 14 days. An expired invitation
can be resent from **Account → Team**; the original link stops working once a
new one is issued.

## Removing someone

Removing a member revokes their tokens immediately and reassigns any content
they owned to the workspace owner. Their comment history is preserved and
remains attributed to them.
